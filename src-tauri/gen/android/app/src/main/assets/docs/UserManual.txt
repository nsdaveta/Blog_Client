BLOGIFY - USER MANUAL

1. INTRODUCTION
Blogify is a desktop application for content creation.

2. GETTING STARTED
- Login with your registered email and password.
- If you haven't registered, use the "Create one" link on the login page.
- Check your email for an OTP to verify your account.

3. CREATING POSTS
- Click "New Post" on the Dashboard.
- Add a Title, some content, and upload an image (optional).
- Click "Publish" to save.

4. UPDATING POSTS
- Use the "Edit" button on your dashboard items.

5. SECURITY
- Always log out if using a shared workstation.
- Do not share your OTP with anyone.

For more information, visit https://blogify-app.pro/docs
